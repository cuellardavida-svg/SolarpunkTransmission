// SOLARPUNK DIALECT

Rimaykullayki Quechua-inspired
Warm greeting. An open door formed by community and sunlight. The language of the network begins here.
Yaku Quechua-inspired
Water, flow. Routing data dynamically to nourish rather than extract.
Mana Quechua-inspired
Boundary. A closed gate circle — the right to refuse, to protect, to hold a line.
Sulpayki Quechua-inspired
Gratitude. Flower-cup receiving a drop of water. Spoken when a node carries your message forward.
Allillanmi Quechua-inspired
Steady well-being. Nested circles with a calm center — the state of a healthy network.

// NETWORK TERMS

Garden Mesh
Distributed frequency band used by cooperative agricultural communes. Warm, fragmented, community-operated.
Tower Spine
Upper-infrastructure relay channel. Colder, more official — prone to suppression and signal injection.
Convergence Node
Point where all channels meet at sunrise. An unstable braid of voices, alarms, and birdsong. Decisions made here propagate outward.
The Infinite Many
A stitched archive of many voices speaking as one. Not a person — a collective memory made transmissible.
Accord §8
The foundational protocol establishing that relay state is not stored by machine. See [ Accord §8 ].

// ENTITIES

Verdant Collective
Agricultural think tank and data nexus. "Verdant" evokes growth and sustainability; "Collective" reflects communal networks and shared stewardship.
Axiom
An androgynous editorial figure. The hidden architect of resistance. References both geometric precision and foundational principles — the truths that govern in a post-truth landscape.
GNN — Global News Network
The dominant media infrastructure. Extractivist by design. Its terminals are also where the resistance hides.
